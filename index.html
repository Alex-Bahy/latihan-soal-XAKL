ELEMENT 1 — SPEAKING (Reading-Based)

Questions 1–8 · Comprehension, Vocabulary, and Inference

Question 01
📌 Context"The treasurer received a payment of Rp 5,000,000 from a client and recorded it as a credit to the company's revenue account in the accounting system."

Speaking context: You are explaining this transaction to a new colleague.
What does the word "CREDIT" mean in this accounting context?

— Penjelasan
Dalam akuntansi, credit (kredit) berarti pencatatan yang menambah akun pendapatan, ekuitas, atau kewajiban. Ketika klien membayar dan dicatat sebagai kredit ke revenue account, artinya perusahaan menerima pemasukan. Ini berbeda dengan penggunaan sehari-hari kata "kredit" (pinjaman). Jawaban A salah karena itu definisi debit. Jawaban C salah karena kredit bukan pinjaman dalam konteks ini.
Question 02
📌 Context"Follow the PROTOCOL below when handling a customer refund: verify the original receipt, confirm the amount, obtain supervisor approval, then process the return."

Speaking context: You are presenting this guide to trainees.
Which word is the CLOSEST synonym for "PROTOCOL" as used in this instruction?

— Penjelasan
Protocol dalam konteks instruksi kerja berarti prosedur/tata cara yang harus diikuti secara berurutan. Sinonimnya yang paling tepat adalah procedure (prosedur). Kata "document" hanya berarti dokumen (benda), bukan urutan langkah. "Permission" = izin, "Penalty" = sanksi, "Output" = hasil — semuanya tidak berkaitan dengan urutan tata cara.
Question 03
📌 ContextRina Pattiwael is a first-year accounting student at SMK Negeri 1 Maluku Tengah. She always prepares her stationery and textbooks the night before school. She uses a digital ledger app to practice recording transactions, and she submits every assignment one day before the deadline. Her classmates often ask for her help when they do not understand journal entries.

Speaking context: You are presenting Rina's profile in class.
Based on the text, which statement is TRUE about Rina?

— Penjelasan
Teks secara eksplisit menyatakan: "She uses a digital ledger app to practice recording transactions." Maka jawaban D adalah BENAR. Jawaban A salah (ia pakai digital, bukan kertas). B salah (ia mengumpulkan tugas sehari sebelum deadline). C salah (teman-temannya meminta bantuan = ia populer). E salah (ia membantu teman yang kesulitan).
Question 04

  • Save the completed voucher to the company's digital archive.
  • Fill in the payment amount, date, and recipient's name.
  • Obtain the supervisor's signature for authorization.
  • Collect the original invoice from the supplier.
  • Prepare a blank payment voucher form.

Speaking context: You must explain this procedure in the correct order to a trainee.
Which sequence represents the CORRECT procedure?

Based on the implicit cues, what can be CONCLUDED about Benny's professional attitude?

Question 06
📌 ContextCitra Marasabessy is a third-year accounting student who joins her school's accounting club every Monday and Wednesday. She practices journal entries for no less than 90 minutes each session and consistently earns the highest scores on accounting lab tests throughout the semester.

Speaking context: You are presenting Citra's character profile to a recruitment panel.
The phrases "every Monday and Wednesday," "no less than 90 minutes," and "consistently" suggest what about Citra?

— Penjelasan
Signal words (kata sinyal) dalam soal ini berfungsi sebagai penanda frekuensi dan konsistensi: "every Monday and Wednesday" = rutinitas teratur; "no less than 90 minutes" = durasi minimum yang konsisten; "consistently" = terus-menerus, tidak berubah. Kombinasi ketiganya menunjukkan komitmen yang teratur dan berdisiplin (option B). Bukan karena terpaksa atau kesulitan belajar.
Question 07
📌 ContextWhen a discrepancy appears in the petty cash report, Wulan does not panic or immediately report to her manager. She first re-counts all physical cash, re-checks the receipt folder, and compares the totals with the ledger before drafting her findings in writing.

Speaking context: You are highlighting Wulan's strengths in a staff appraisal presentation.
Based on the action words and context clues, what can be concluded about Wulan's working style?

— Penjelasan
Kata kerja aksi dalam teks: re-counts, re-checks, compares — semua menunjukkan proses verifikasi bertahap sebelum mengambil kesimpulan. Ini adalah ciri gaya kerja yang sistematis dan teliti. Jawaban A salah karena teks tidak menunjukkan rasa takut. E jelas salah karena Wulan justru sangat hati-hati, bukan impulsif. D bersifat opini negatif yang tidak didukung teks.
Question 08
📌 Context"Andi Tuasikal is a second-year accounting student who tutors his juniors every Friday afternoon. He spends at least two hours each day solving practice problems from accounting textbooks. He also reads the latest government financial regulation updates published online every month."

Speaking context: In a debate, you must defend the MOST LOGICAL inference about Andi.
Which inference about Andi is the MOST LOGICAL and supported by the STRONGEST evidence?

— Penjelasan
Inferensi yang baik harus: (1) logis dan (2) didukung oleh bukti teks yang kuat. Opsi B didukung TIGA bukti spesifik: mengajar junior setiap Jumat (mengajar = pemahaman mendalam), berlatih 2 jam/hari (ketekunan), dan membaca regulasi terbaru (up-to-date). Opsi A hanya asumsi dari satu fakta. D dan E adalah asumsi negatif tanpa dukungan teks. Inferensi yang baik = banyak bukti + logika yang kuat.

ELEMENT 2 — READING & VIEWING

Questions 9–18 · Vocabulary in Context, Infographic Analysis, and Critical Reading

Question 09
📌 Context"After the employee submitted her reimbursement claim, the finance department verified the receipts and the approved amount was credited back to her salary account."

What does "CREDITED BACK" mean in the sentence above?

— Penjelasan
"Credited back" = dikembalikan/ditambahkan kembali ke akun. Kata "back" menunjukkan pengembalian uang (reimbursement), dan "credited" menunjukkan penambahan ke akun. Jadi, uang yang telah dikeluarkan karyawan dikembalikan ke rekeningnya. Ini bukan pengurangan (A), bukan penolakan (B), bukan penalti (D), dan bukan pinjaman (E).
Question 10
📌 Context"The annual inventory count was LABORIOUS because the warehouse contained thousands of items categorized across twenty different sections, and each item required individual verification against the purchase order."

Based on the context clues, what is the most likely meaning of "LABORIOUS"?

— Penjelasan
Petunjuk konteks: "ribuan item di 20 bagian berbeda" + "setiap item diverifikasi satu per satu" = pekerjaan yang sangat banyak dan membutuhkan waktu lama. Maka laborious = membutuhkan usaha dan waktu besar (requiring great effort and time). Akar kata: labor = kerja keras. A salah (justru tidak otomatis). E berlawanan langsung. B dan C tidak relevan dengan konteks.
Question 11
📌 ContextHendra Lesnussa is a third-year accounting student who prepares a summary of weekly cash flow every Sunday evening. He attends every accounting workshop offered by his school without missing a single session. He also voluntarily creates visual summaries of financial statements for his classmates.

Which of the following is an EXPLICITLY STATED (explicit) fact about Hendra?

— Penjelasan
Fakta eksplisit = informasi yang SECARA LANGSUNG disebutkan dalam teks, bukan interpretasi. Opsi A adalah satu-satunya yang dikutip langsung dari teks: "prepares a summary of weekly cash flow every Sunday evening." Opsi B, C, D, E adalah inferensi atau asumsi yang tidak disebutkan secara langsung. Penting: dalam soal reading, bedakan antara yang TERTULIS dan yang DISIMPULKAN.

Question 13
📌 ContextA junior accountant follows these steps to prepare the monthly payroll:
  • Collect attendance data from the HR department.
  • Calculate gross salary for each employee.
  • Click "Process Payroll" in the payroll software.
  • Distribute payslips to all employees.
The payroll officer later discovers that tax deductions were never applied.

Which MISSING step most likely caused this error?

— Penjelasan
Setelah menghitung gaji bruto (gross salary), langkah standar payroll adalah: potong pajak/BPJS → hitung gaji bersih (net salary) → baru proses payroll. Langkah antara step 2 dan 3 yang hilang adalah penerapan potongan pajak dan perhitungan gaji bersih. Inilah yang menyebabkan pajak tidak teraplikasi. A, D, E tidak didukung teks. C secara logis tidak mungkin (payslip diberikan setelah payroll diproses).
Question 14
📌 Accounting Cycle Diagram

Identify → Record → Post → [Trial Balance SKIPPED] → Adjusting Entries → Financial Statements → Closing

A company skips the Trial Balance step to save time. What would MOST LIKELY happen as a result?

— Penjelasan
Trial Balance berfungsi sebagai "filter kesalahan" — memastikan total debit = total kredit sebelum melanjutkan. Jika dilewati, kesalahan pencatatan tidak akan terdeteksi dan akan ikut terbawa ke laporan keuangan, menyebabkan laporan menjadi tidak akurat. Adjusting entries (A) tidak dirancang untuk mendeteksi ketidakseimbangan debit-kredit — itu bukan fungsinya. D dan C adalah asumsi yang tidak berdasar.

Question 16

Which reason BEST explains why the visual diagram is more effective for teaching this topic?

Semua ini mempercepat pemahaman dan memperkuat daya ingat. Opsi B merangkum fungsi-fungsi ini dengan tepat. Question 17
📌 Accounting Cycle Diagram

Identify → Record → Post → Trial Balance → [Adjusting Entries SKIPPED] → Financial Statements → Closing

The accountant skips Adjusting Entries believing all journal entries are already complete.

Which analysis BEST explains the impact of this decision?

— Penjelasan
Adjusting Entries (Jurnal Penyesuaian) berfungsi untuk mencatat item-item yang belum terekam: beban yang sudah terjadi tapi belum dibayar (accrued expenses), biaya dibayar di muka (prepaid), dan penyusutan (depreciation). Jika dilewati, laporan keuangan akan melebih-lebihkan pendapatan dan meremehkan beban, sehingga laba bersih yang ditampilkan TIDAK AKURAT. Trial balance (A) hanya memverifikasi bahwa debit = kredit — bukan memastikan kelengkapan pencatatan.

ELEMENT 3 — WRITING & PRESENTING

Questions 19–25 · Terminology, Sequencing, Inference, and Character Analysis

Question 19
📌 Context"A vendor delivered office supplies worth Rp 3,200,000. The company provided the vendor with a printed document listing the items delivered, their quantities, and the total amount owed by the company."

In writing a financial report, what is the correct term for the document SENT BY THE VENDOR to request payment?

— Penjelasan
Invoice (faktur) = dokumen yang dikirim oleh penjual/vendor kepada pembeli berisi daftar barang/jasa, jumlah, dan total tagihan yang harus dibayar. Berbeda dengan: Receipt = bukti bahwa pembayaran SUDAH diterima; Voucher = bukti internal transaksi; Balance Sheet = laporan posisi keuangan; Journal Entry = pencatatan transaksi dalam buku jurnal. Kunci: invoice = tagihan dari vendor, receipt = bukti bayar sudah lunas.
Question 20
📌 Context"The presentation slide uses BLUE color for completed tasks, ORANGE color for tasks currently in progress, and RED color for tasks that are overdue and require urgent resolution."

In the context of designing a project progress presentation, what does the ORANGE color indicate?

— Penjelasan
Teks secara eksplisit mendefinisikan: BIRU = selesai, ORANYE = sedang dikerjakan (in progress), MERAH = terlambat/urgent. Membaca pertanyaan tentang kode warna = membaca LEGENDA dengan cermat dan mencocokkan warna dengan definisinya. Jawaban B (terlambat) = definisi MERAH, bukan oranye. Hindari menukar definisi antar warna.
Question 21
📌 Context"To activate your prepaid debit card, you must first RELOAD your card by visiting an authorized agent or transferring funds through internet banking. The minimum reload amount is Rp 50,000."

In the context of writing a digital banking guide, what does "RELOAD" mean?

— Penjelasan
Reload (dalam konteks kartu prabayar/e-wallet) = mengisi ulang saldo kartu dengan cara mentransfer dana. Petunjuk konteks: "visiting an authorized agent or transferring funds" = cara mengisi saldo. Ini sama dengan istilah "top-up" yang umum digunakan di Indonesia. A (tarik tunai), C (konversi mata uang), D (batalkan kartu), E (kirim ke orang lain) — semuanya BERBEDA dari mengisi saldo kartu sendiri.
